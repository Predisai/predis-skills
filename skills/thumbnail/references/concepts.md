# Thumbnail concepts

A thumbnail has one job: make someone curious enough to click, then the video pays it off. The image asks a question. The title and the video answer it.

**Truth rule:** you may exaggerate, never misrepresent. No results, people, quotes, numbers, screens or products that are not in the video.

## Method

1. Write the video's true promise in one line: "I tried X for 30 days and Y happened."
2. Brainstorm at least 6 concepts from different patterns below. Keep this internal.
3. Keep the 3 strongest. Each has one focal subject, one open question, and reads in under a second on a phone.
4. Show them as three short lines: a name, the pattern in plain words, and 1 to 4 words of text (or "no text").

## Patterns

| Pattern | How it looks |
|---|---|
| Big face | Subject fills 40 to 60% of the frame, chest-up, one strong emotion, almost nothing behind. Default when a person is the story |
| Before / after | One subject in two states, high contrast. The only pattern that uses a split |
| Frozen moment | One intriguing instant mid-action: the jump, the pour, the crash |
| Size contrast | Something huge beside something tiny, both part of the story |
| Too many | The frame packed with one object, plus one person for scale |
| One thing louder | The real scene with one true element made bigger or brighter |
| Product as answer | The product large and sharp; the question is "what does it do" |
| Progression | Three panels left to right: start, middle, end |
| Day badge | Any pattern plus a bold "DAY 27" style badge |
| Map or route | Aerial or map view with one highlighted path or pin |
| Place as hero | The location is the star; one small person on a third for scale |
| Simple chart | One clean familiar graphic, like a line going up |
| Plain card | A generic chat bubble or star-rating card with a short true line. No real app branding |
| Arrow or circle | One mark on the detail that matters, plus 1 to 2 words |

Combine two only if the result still reads in one second.

## Text

- 1 to 4 words, bold, all caps or title case. It adds what the image cannot say. It never repeats the title.
- Good: `IT WORKED?`, `DAY 30`, `DON'T BUY THIS`. Bad: a sentence, or the title again.
- Keep it off faces, usually top or one side. Never bottom-right on 16:9 (the video length badge covers it).
- One color plus a thick outline or shadow. Yellow or white on dark, black on bright.

## Faces

One emotion per take, described physically: "eyebrows raised high, mouth open, eyes wide". Options: shock, hype, awe, fear, laughter, smugness, disgust, confusion, determination.

## Layout

- One hero on a third, or centered for a big face. Clear size order.
- Subject separates from the background with rim light, color contrast or blur.
- Bright, saturated, high contrast, unless the channel's style is muted.
- Safe zones: 16:9 keep the bottom-right clear. 9:16 keep faces and text in the upper two-thirds and away from the right edge (app buttons sit there). 4:5 keep a 10% margin on every side.
